STORY LYRICS — MILESTONE 4 COMPLETION NOTES

Completed: Story Background and Direct Manipulation Tools

Implemented:
- Drag lyric layer directly on the Story canvas
- Two-finger pinch scaling and rotation
- Mouse-wheel scaling and Shift+wheel rotation
- Reset and centre controls
- Instagram Story-style top, bottom, horizontal, and vertical safe guides
- Toggleable guide overlay
- Background image and looping video support
- Background blur and dim controls
- Five gradient-overlay choices
- Fill and Fit background modes
- Video-background start-position control
- Responsive mobile editor refinements

The effect names, assets, controls, and code remain original. The interaction model is deliberately close to a familiar Story composer without copying Instagram branding or proprietary assets.
