## Instagram Lyrics

Instagram Lyrics is the canonical replacement for the removed Stroke effect. Its typography is intentionally integrated with the shared KEFE typography contract rather than maintaining a separate font implementation.

The treatment is based on Instagram Stories Music lyrics: compact bold sans-serif lettering, uppercase presentation, tight leading, a dominant active lyric, quieter neighbouring lyrics, and smooth handoff between lyric states. It uses no outline, stroke, glow or typewriter animation.

Do not substitute fonts or alter these assignments without an explicit design decision.
