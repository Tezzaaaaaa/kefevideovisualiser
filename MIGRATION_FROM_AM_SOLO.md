# Migration from AM Solo

AM Solo 1.0 is preserved in `legacy/AM_Solo_v1.0.zip`.

Reusable components:
- Go HTTP service structure
- authenticated local-network patterns
- media upload and storage concepts
- metadata/artwork models
- iPhone companion foundations

Removed from the new public direction:
- Apple Music URL parsing
- downloader terminology
- simulated Apple Music download jobs

The new product handles audio, lyrics and artwork supplied by users who own or are authorised to use them.
